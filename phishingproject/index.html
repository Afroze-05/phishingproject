Stay Safe From Phishing

Follow these simple rules to protect yourself online.

🔗
Don't click blindly
Verify links before opening them. Hover over links to see the actual destination.
🔐
Never share OTP
Banks and legitimate services should not ask you to share OTPs or passwords.
📧
Verify the sender
Check the actual email address, not just the displayed name. Watch for misspellings.
⏱️
Don't panic
Phishing attacks often create urgency. Stop and verify first before taking any action.
